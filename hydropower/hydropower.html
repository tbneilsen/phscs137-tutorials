Hydroelectric Power: Water, Gravity, and Electricity

1. How does a hydroelectric dam generate electricity?

Water at a higher elevation has gravitational potential energy. Flowing down a penstock, it turns a turbine connected to a generator. The effective head is the elevation-related energy difference between upstream and downstream water, not necessarily the dam's full height.

Hydropower uses no combustion fuel during operation, but dams can change fish migration, sediment transport, river habitats, and land use.
