<h1>Battery-Power Tutorial</h1>
<div class="panel"><h2>1. Understand the analogy</h2><p>Imagine a tank supplying water through a main pipe that divides into branches. Each branch serves a different device. Opening a valve is like plugging in and turning on a device.</p><p><strong>Water flow rate (L/min)</strong> represents electrical <strong>power (W)</strong>. <strong>Water stored in the tank (L)</strong> represents battery <strong>energy (Wh)</strong>. The main pipe's <strong>maximum flow capacity</strong> represents the power station's <strong>maximum output rating</strong>.</p><p class="foot">This is a conceptual analogy, not a detailed hydraulic or electrical circuit model. We assume each open branch draws a fixed flow rate, provided the source can supply the total.</p></div>
