How Long Will a Battery Last?

Interactive energy and power tutorial · About 15–20 minutes

1. Energy and power are different

Energy is how much is available or used. Power is how quickly energy is transferred or used.

Imagine a water tank and a hose. The amount of water in the tank is like stored energy. The rate at which water flows through the hose is like power.

Energy = Power × Time   (E = P × t)

A 100 W device running for 2 hours uses 200 Wh of energy. A watt (W) is one joule per second; a watt-hour (Wh) is a unit of energy.
